# Storage, Dosage & Frequency

## Storage
Keep your shrooms in a cool, dark place away from moisture, children and pets.

## Dosage
Take one capsule (0.1g) every third day on an empty stomach in the morning.

## Frequency
For best results, leave a minimum 72 hours in between doses to avoid building a tolerance to the medicine; remain disciplined with the dosage frequency; and fulfil the full 12 week course.
