# The Benefits

Many studies, both ancient and recent, have validated mushrooms as a treatment for several disorders. Particular progress has been seen in the treatment of depression, anxiety and addiction recovery.

Studies have also identified beneficial effects of psilocybin on neurological conditions such as ADHD, dementia and Parkinson's disease. Research has even seen treatment for cancers.

Reports include benefits such as:
- Increased focus
- Improved attention span
- Improvements in general cognitive flexibility
- Heightened awareness
- Creativity
- Improvement in mood

When combined with regular, larger doses the results can be dramatic; potentially life changing.
