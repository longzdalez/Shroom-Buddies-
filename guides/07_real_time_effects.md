# Real-Time Effects

Although you should perceive no major effects while micro-dosing, you may experience improved focus, feel calmer, more decisive or generally less stressed/worried than usual. You will most likely forget that you have taken it on dose days, but more and more you will look back and see that your days have been getting better.

This is normal and it means that it is working. This is the realisation phase which you must first mentally acknowledge before integrating into your daily thoughts as much as you can.

## Microdose
noun  /ˈmaɪ.kroʊ.doʊs/
An extremely small amount of a substance typically intended to produce no perceptible effects whilst over time potentially producing subtle or cumulative effects.
