# Longer-Term Effects

If you want to get the most out of the experience, the process is simple. The more you recognise positive effects and changes that occur, the more your confidence in the medicine increases.

The more you believe that it is working, the more it works, and so on and so forth.

This is the basic mechanic of how the medicine heals you at a thought level. Your thoughts are your reality; if you want change, your thoughts and actions have to change first.

Unlike larger trip doses, the healing happens without you being aware in real-time. Your neural networks will build communications that have previously been silent. The effects will be imperceptible at first but if you really listen, you will become more aware of the things that really matter.

Micro-dosing could be your first step on the path to contentment and connection within yourself; to building those connections with everything around you. These are not new abilities, but ones that have always lain within you. Magic mushrooms simply show you where they are and how to use them.
