# Support/Aftercare

Whilst support mechanisms in public fields are limited, there are groups who specialise in the use of psychoactive substances, however they are typically concerned with the processing of a psychedelic experience rather than the guidance or facilitation of a regular dosing program.

As psychedelics' popularity increases, so does the need for proper support systems. At Shroom Buddies, we are creating a support network, outside of the pharmaceutical system, which seeks to educate - not to monetise.

In truth, the support network starts here. With you and with us. We are aiming to build a community that acts as the perfect support group through sharing experiences with one another - this is something we would like every one of you involved in.

Our team is committed to uncovering, exploring and sharing information in the field of psychedelic substance use to raise awareness and promote healthy and safe use.

Information surrounding the use of psychoactive substances for medical purposes has been hidden deep below the surface of medical research for too long - Shroom Buddies are seeking to bring out the truth about psilocybin and its abilities; sharing knowledge, and creating acknowledgement, realisation and belief.

It's up to us from here.
