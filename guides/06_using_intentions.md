# Using Intentions

It is important that you set intentions and adhere to them during your course.

Visualise what it is you are trying to achieve. Whether it be physical, mental or emotional, you should practise bringing your mind back to these ways of thinking as often as you can.

Actively practise being a better person. Envisage yourself as the person you want to be. Treat others with the same morals and standards as you wish to be treated yourself. All of the above and more.

Be gentle with yourself. Number 1 intention.

Practise empathy and understanding as a priority.

Practise gratefulness for all the good you have in your life.

The more you experience these feelings the more of these feelings you will experience.

The shrooms are an amplifier and facilitate this process in a natural, intuitive way.
