# How It Works

Psilocybin is the active ingredient in magic mushrooms. When ingested, the body converts it to psilocin. This activates serotonin receptors 5-HT2A in the brain, triggering a molecular signalling cascade inside the neurones and strengthening their connections with one another.

Neuroplasticity increases; thoughts become more flexible and less predictable; previously disconnected regions of the brain begin to communicate and create new neurological pathways whilst rerouting old thoughts, habits and mental processes that don't serve you anymore.

Activity in the Default Mode Network (DMN) decreases. This is the network of pathways responsible for your inner most thought processes - the ego. Your nemesis. This is what holds you back.

Deeply held emotions are revisited, re-evaluated and put to bed - processed, at last, using new ways of thinking.

Magic mushrooms show you how to rebuild your inner narrative from an intuitive point of view - shifting the focus. Reconstructing how you perceive the world that you create.

Where they come from, there are no words, as none are needed. Only thoughts and actions are real, and such is your reality.
