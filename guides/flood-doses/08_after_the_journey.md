# After the Journey

The journey is the seed. Integration is how it grows.

In the days and weeks afterwards your mind is more open and flexible than usual. This is the perfect time to make changes stick.

- **Write it down** as soon as you can, before the details fade
- **Talk it through** with your sitter or someone you trust
- **Spend time in nature**, rest and eat well
- **Take small actions** that match what you learned
- **Give it time.** Some insights only make sense weeks later.

Use the Journal and Today pages in this app to track how your mood and energy change in the weeks that follow.

If you feel low, unsettled or not like yourself for more than a few days, reach out and talk to someone.
