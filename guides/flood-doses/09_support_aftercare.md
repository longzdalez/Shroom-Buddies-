# Support/Aftercare

You're never on your own.

## Get help straight away (call 999) if someone:
- Has a seizure or chest pain
- Collapses or can't be woken
- Is in danger of hurting themselves or others
- Is extremely distressed and can't be calmed

Be honest with paramedics about what was taken. They're there to help, not judge.

## For support afterwards
- **Samaritans:** 116 123, free, any time
- **FRANK (UK drugs advice):** 0300 123 6600 or talktofrank.com
- **TripSit:** tripsit.me, live chat during or after a journey
- Your GP, or a therapist who understands psychedelic integration

## The Shroom Buddies community
Share your experiences, lean on each other and keep each other safe. That's what we're here for.

A note: psilocybin mushrooms are a Class A drug in the UK. Please know the law where you live.

It's up to us from here.
