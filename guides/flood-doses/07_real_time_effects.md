# Real-Time Effects

## Coming up
Usually within 20 to 40 minutes. You may feel restless, yawny or a little queasy. This passes. Breathe slowly and get comfortable.

## The peak
Around 1 to 2 hours in. Colours brighten, music feels alive, time stretches and thoughts flow in new directions. Emotions can come in waves: laughter, tears, awe. All of it is normal.

## Winding down
Effects usually fade over 4 to 6 hours. You'll feel tired, open and reflective.

## If it gets difficult
Challenging moments are part of the process, not a sign that something has gone wrong.
- Remind yourself: *this is the mushrooms, and it will pass*
- Breathe slowly and deeply
- Change the music or the room
- Let your sitter hold your hand
- Don't fight it. Lean in, and let it move through you.
