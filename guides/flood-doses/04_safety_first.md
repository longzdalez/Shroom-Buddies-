# Safety First

## Who should NOT take a flood dose
- Anyone with a personal or family history of psychosis, schizophrenia or bipolar disorder
- Anyone with heart problems or high blood pressure, as psilocybin raises both
- Anyone with epilepsy or a history of seizures
- Pregnant or breastfeeding women

## Medication warnings
- **Lithium:** never combine. It has been linked to seizures.
- **MAOIs and tramadol:** avoid. Serious interaction risks.
- **Antidepressants (SSRIs/SNRIs):** usually dull the effects. Never stop prescribed medication without talking to your doctor.
- Don't mix with alcohol or other drugs.

## Dosage
Mushrooms vary hugely in strength, even from the same batch, so we don't give amounts here. If you're unsure, start lower than you think. You can't undo a dose once it's taken. For trusted, up-to-date dosage and testing advice, see **tripsit.me**, **dancesafe.org** or **psychonautwiki.org**.

## Storage
Keep your shrooms in a cool, dark, dry place, well away from children and pets.

## Never drive
Not on the day, and not until you feel completely yourself again.
