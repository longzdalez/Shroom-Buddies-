# The Benefits

Science is catching up with what ancient cultures have known for centuries.

Clinical trials have shown that psilocybin given with professional support can bring lasting relief for:
- Treatment-resistant depression
- Anxiety and distress in people facing serious illness
- Addiction, including alcohol and smoking

In the US, psilocybin therapy for depression is now very close to approval, after two large positive trials. In the UK it's still being studied and isn't available on the NHS yet.

People also report:
- A shift in perspective and priorities
- Releasing long-held emotions
- Feeling more connected and compassionate
- A renewed sense of meaning

Remember, the trials use careful screening and trained therapists. That support is a big part of why they work, which is why preparation, a sitter and aftercare matter so much.
