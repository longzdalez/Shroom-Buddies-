# How It Works

Psilocybin is turned into psilocin by your body. Psilocin switches on the brain's 5-HT2A serotonin receptors, and that's when things start to shift.

The Default Mode Network (the part of the brain that runs your inner voice and your sense of "me") goes quiet. With the ego turned down, areas of the brain that rarely talk to each other start communicating.

That's why a journey can bring:
- Vivid colours, patterns and visions
- Deep emotions rising to the surface
- Fresh perspective on old problems
- A feeling of connection to people, nature and everything around you

Afterwards, the brain stays more flexible for a while. This "window" is where the real work happens: new habits and new ways of thinking are easier to build.
