# Using Intentions

An intention is your compass for the journey.

Before you begin, ask yourself: *What do I want to understand, heal or let go of?*

Keep it simple and open:
- "Show me what I need to see."
- "Help me understand my grief."
- "I want to be kinder to myself."

Write it down. Read it before you start. Then let it go and trust the experience.

The journey may not give you what you asked for. It often gives you what you need instead.

If things get intense, come back to your breath and your intention.

Be gentle with yourself. Number 1 intention, always.
