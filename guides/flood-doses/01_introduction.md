# Introduction

Welcome to the deeper end.

A flood dose (sometimes called a macro dose or "journey") is a full, noticeable psilocybin experience. Unlike micro-dosing, you will feel it, and for a few hours your world will look, sound and feel very different.

People take this step for healing, insight, grief, creativity or simply to reconnect with themselves. Used with care, respect and good preparation, it can be one of the most meaningful days of your life.

Used carelessly, it can be frightening.

This guide is here to help you do it the safe way: who it's not for, how to prepare, what to expect, and how to look after yourself afterwards.

Go gently. There's no rush.
