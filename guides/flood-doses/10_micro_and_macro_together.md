# Micro & Macro Together

Microdosing and flood doses aren't rivals. For some people, they work beautifully side by side.

## The difference
Microdosing is the gentle, everyday nudge. You barely notice it, but over weeks it can lift your mood, sharpen your focus and soften old habits.

A flood dose is the deep dive. One day, a few hours, and you can see your life from a completely new angle.

## Why combine them?
- The journey shows you *what* needs to change
- Microdosing helps you *live* those changes day to day
- Your intentions run through both, so they keep pulling in the same direction

Think of the journey as the map, and microdosing as the walk.

## Training the mind
Every time you sit down with intention, reflect and integrate, you're practising a new way of thinking. The more regularly and mindfully you work with it, the more natural that way of thinking becomes.

Like any training, consistency and integration matter more than intensity.

## Go gently
- Start with smaller, gentler experiences and get to know how your mind and body respond
- There's no rush to go deeper. Bigger isn't always better, and many people get everything they need from gentle work
- Leave plenty of time between journeys to rest and integrate what you've learned
- Only step up when you feel ready, settled and supported, never because you feel you should
- Always follow the Safety First page, and check tripsit.me or psychonautwiki.org for up-to-date dose information

## Is it for you?
Combining the two works well for some people and not for others. Use the Calendar graph to see how your mood and energy respond over time, and listen to what it shows you.

Be gentle with yourself. Number 1 intention.
