# Preparing Yourself

With a flood dose, preparation is everything. Psychedelic guides call it **set and setting**.

## Set (your mindset)
Choose a time when you feel steady. Avoid journeys when you're in crisis, very stressed or not in a good place. Know why you're doing it, and write your intention down.

## Setting (your space)
Somewhere safe, private and comfortable. Soft lighting, blankets, water, a bucket nearby just in case, and calm music. Phones off, no plans, nowhere to be.

## Your sitter
Never journey alone. Have a trusted, sober friend with you for the whole experience. Their job isn't to guide you, just to keep you safe, keep you company and remind you that you're OK.

## The next day
Keep it free. Give yourself time to rest and reflect.
