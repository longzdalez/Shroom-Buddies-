# Preparing Yourself

Minimal preparation is needed when micro-dosing as you will feel little/no noticeable physical effects immediately after taking the medicine.

You do not have to be in a particular frame of mind when micro-dosing. Over time, you should begin to see a shift in mindset and general wellbeing.

Micro-dosing is perfect practice for allowing your mind to be open to new ideas or insights that you may experience once you have become accustomed to the course.

Change how you think and what you're thinking about will change.

Psilocybin helps with this process.
